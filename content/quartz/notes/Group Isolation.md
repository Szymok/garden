## 👽 Brudnopis
It is possible to create Isolated Groups or branches, which consist Of an isolated group and its children. When a group is isolated, members of the group or branch are restricted from sharing assets and assigning stewardship to assets outside Of their group or branch.
Members of an isolated group or branch can only Share assets with members Of said isolated group or branch. Members of an isolated group or branch can only assign stewardship to groups within the isolated branch. Users in two isolated branches can share assets within both branches unless stewardship is already assigned to one of the branches, Sharing with individual users who are members Of parent groups Of the isolated branch is allowed. Stewardship assignment and sharing With the entire group must Stay within the isolated branch to avoid passing access outside its hierarchy. Assets shared with users outside the branch before isolation remain unaffected; manual revocation is needed if necessary. Sharing and assigning stewardship by users from parent groups and external groups are not affected
