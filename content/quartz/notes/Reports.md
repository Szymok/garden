## 👽 Brudnopis
With Reports, you can manage all BI reports on catalog items that you build in your Bl tool.
The connection between ONE and your Bl tool enables you to:
- Display, store and explore data reports in one place.
- Visualize data in several ways.
- Schedule periodical report updates to get the most up-to-date information on your data.
#### Manage Reports
In ONE :
- You can preview reports, adjust the visuals, and configure metadata changes.
- You cannot change the report content or structure; this can only be done from the Bl tool where this report was built.
To View Reports go to Data Catalog > Catalog Items > Reports:
- Search for reports by their status, applied terms, other parameters, or using full-text search.
- Preview reports, change the visuals, and configure metadata changes in ONE.
