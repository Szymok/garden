Ił should always be remembered that the machine can, in some forms and by these new means, produce algorithmic choices. What the machine does is a technical choice among several possibilities and is based either on well-defined criteria or on statistical inference. The human being, on the other hand, not only chooses, but in his or her heart is capable of deciding. The decision is What we might call a more strategic element of a choice and requires practical evaluation. Sometimes, often in the difficult łask of governing, we are called upon to make decisions with consequences for many other persons as well.
