Meity invites proposals to develop trusted AI ecosystem

PTILast Updated: Dec 17, 2024, 01:39:00 PM IST

Synopsis

## This proposal includes creation of "deepfake detection tools to enable real-time identification and mitigation of deepfakes, preventing misinformation and harm for a secure and trustworthy digital ecosystem". The ministry has also invited proposals for watermarking and labelling tools to authenticate AI-generated content, ensuring it is traceable, secure, and free of harmful materials.

![Unesco MeitY partner to assess gaps in ethical AI use in India](https://img.etimg.com/thumb/msid-116394093,width-650,height-488,imgsize-6310,resizemode-75/unesco-meity-partner-to-assess-gaps-in-ethical-ai-use-in-india.jpg)

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (Meity) has invited proposals from entities for the development of technology tools to create a trusted AI ecosystem, including the detection of deepfakes, as per information published on [Meity](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/meity)'s website. As part of [IndiaAI mission](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/indiaai-mission), the Safe and Trusted AI pillar envisages the development of indigenous tools and frameworks and self-assessment checklists for innovators, among others, to put in place adequate guardrails to advance the responsible adoption of AI.

"To spearhead this movement, IndiaAI is calling for Expressions of Interest (EOI) from individuals and organisations that want to lead AI development projects to foster accountability, mitigate AI harms and promote fairness in [AI practices](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/ai-practices)," the note for proposal said.

Miety has invited proposals for watermarking and labelling tools to authenticate AI-generated content, ensuring it is traceable, secure, and free of harmful materials.

The proposal calls for the need to establish [AI frameworks](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/ai-frameworks) that align with global standards, ensuring AI respects human values and promotes fairness.

The proposal includes the creation of "[deepfake detection tools](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/deepfake-detection-tools) to enable real-time identification and mitigation of deepfakes, preventing misinformation and harm for a secure and trustworthy digital ecosystem".
