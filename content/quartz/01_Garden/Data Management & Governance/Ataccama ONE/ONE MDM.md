- The [[Ataccama|Ataccama]] ONE MDM suite is available in **two variants**: **MDM** or **[[RDM|RDM]]**.
- These variants are installed **only in separate environments**.
- For these cases, **installation sizing requirements are reduced** based on customer needs, typically to one or two server instances and an orchestrator.
- **[[Ataccama|Ataccama]] ONE is not deployed in this scenario**.
- **Important Note**: If you require **both MDM and [[RDM|RDM]] within the same environment**, the **only supported option is to install the entire [[Ataccama|Ataccama]] ONE Platform** (which includes ONE, MDM, and [[RDM|RDM]]).
