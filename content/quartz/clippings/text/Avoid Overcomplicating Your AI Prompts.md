**Avoid Overcomplicating Your AI Prompts**

AI is getting smarter. So the prompts we use.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*UVjaS_Cg8uE2MrQVrEqb6Q.png)

If you’re still writing long, step-by-step instructions for AI, you might be **holding it back** rather than helping.

**Newer models like OpenAI o1 and DeepSeek R1 already “think” before responding** — so we need to update our old habits because they may not work as well.

**✅ How to Write Better Prompts Today:**

🔹 **Keep it simple** — Short, clear prompts work best. Too much detail can confuse the model.  
🔹 **Be direct** — Skip unnecessary words. “Summarize X” is better than “Can you please explain X?”  
🔹 **Specify format** — Need a table, JSON, or bullet points? Say so upfront.  
🔹 **Encourage reasoning** — Instead of “What’s 15% of 200?”, try “Explain step by step how to calculate 15% of 200.”  
🔹 **Give context** — AI isn’t a mind reader. A hint or reference improves accuracy.  
🔹 **Use delimiters** — Structure your prompts with **###** or XML tags to make them clearer.  
🔹 **Don’t overfeed RAG models** — Too much context slows them down. Only provide what’s needed.

**🛠 Example: How to Fix a Bad Prompt**

🚫 **Traditional Prompt:**  
*“Can you please write a detailed blog post about the benefits of AI in business, making sure it covers efficiency, cost savings, and innovation, and ensure it’s engaging and easy to read?”*

✅ **Improved Prompt:**  
**“Write a 500-word blog post on the benefits of AI in business. Cover:**

- **Efficiency improvements**
- **Cost savings**
- **Innovation**  
Use a **conversational tone** and format the response in **short paragraphs with bullet points.**”

This “new” version is:  
✅ **More direct** — Removes unnecessary words like “Can you please…”  
✅ **Structured** — Lists key topics clearly  
✅ **Specific** — Defines tone, length, and formatting for better output

💡 **The Bottom Line:** The game has changed. **AI no longer needs to be spoon-fed — it needs clarity.** Better prompts save time, improve results, and push AI beyond its limits.
